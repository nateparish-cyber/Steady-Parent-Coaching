Write your lede here — one tight paragraph that hooks the reader. This becomes
the `<p class="lede">` element at the top of the post, set in a larger, warmer
font than the body. Make it count.

## A heading goes here

Body prose in normal markdown. Use **bold** when you mean it, _italics_ for
emphasis, and [links](https://example.com) generously — internal links to other
posts on the blog are especially good for SEO. Cross-link freely.

You can use:

- Bulleted lists
- For when the rhythm calls for it
- Keep them tight

Or numbered ones:

1. When the order matters
2. Like a sequence of steps
3. Or a small framework

### Subheadings work too

Three levels is plenty. Don't go deeper than `###` — the reader doesn't need it
and Google doesn't care.

> Block quotes get a warm left border and italics — use them sparingly for
> impact, not as a default callout style.

## Another heading

Closing thought lives here. Aim for posts in the **800–1,200 word** range —
short enough to read in one sitting, long enough to rank on Google for the
phrases parents actually type at 10 p.m.
