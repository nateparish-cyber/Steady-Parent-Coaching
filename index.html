The problem

You've read the books.
You know what you "should" do.
It still falls apart at 8pm.

Parenting an anxious child is exhausting in a specific way. You love them completely — and you're stuck in a cycle that makes things worse, even when you're trying to help.

🔁
The reassurance loop
You answer the same worried question eight times. It works for five minutes. Then they're back. The anxiety isn't shrinking — it's learning.
🌙
Bedtime hostage situations
You stay until they fall asleep. Every night. You tell yourself it's just this once. It's been six months.
🏫
The school avoidance spiral
Every morning is a war. You let them stay home once. Now you're negotiating daily. The world is getting smaller for both of you.
😤
You lose it too
Their meltdown triggers yours. You say things you regret. The guilt compounds. You know that's not who you want to be.
🕐
Therapy is once a week
The hard moments happen Tuesday at 7pm — not during Thursday's session. You need support when it's actually happening.
💔
The accommodation trap
Everything you do to ease their discomfort makes sense in the moment. And every accommodation teaches their brain that the world is too dangerous to face.
